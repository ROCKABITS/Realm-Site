## Find a noticeboard

Small, covered, and wide noticeboards open the same contract system. Craft the small board with three papers across the top and six oak planks below. Covered and wide variants have their own recipes in the full guide.

Interact with the board and stay in the same dimension, within eight blocks of the intact structure. Sessions expire after five minutes; reopen the board when needed. `/contracts` no longer remotely opens the menu.

## Complete a material contract

Read the requested item, quantity, total reward, tax, and net payment. The author reserves the reward when publishing.

Deliver **all requested materials at once**. Completion creates a reward credit for you to collect at the board, while the author collects the delivered items. You cannot complete your own private material contract.

An announced reward may still be waiting for collection; it is not necessarily already in your wallet.

## Publish a request

Choose the item identifier, quantity, and reward. Review any policy supplied by Treasury before publishing. The tax rate is recorded at publication, and collecting a completed reward does not charge that tax a second time.

## Daily quests

The Daily Quests area offers **five RNG quests per player**: two easy, two medium, and one hard. The daily rotation renews at **00:00 UTC**.

Accept a quest before doing the work; earlier actions do not count as its progress. The initial gross daily reward limit is 300 €. Quest requirements, rewards, and limits can be configured by administrators.

## Companion mods

Origins supplies digital money and reputation. Treasury supplies tax rules and publicly funded contracts. Material contracts retain an emerald fallback; daily quests only use emerald mode when an administrator enables it.

See the [contract guide](https://github.com/ROCKABITS/Mods/blob/main/contracts-bounties/README.md) and [daily quest rules](https://github.com/ROCKABITS/Mods/blob/main/contracts-bounties/DAILY_QUESTS.md) for configuration, cancellation, refunds, and supported activities.
