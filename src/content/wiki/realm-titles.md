## Find the Royal Seal Desk

All public browsing and appointment management begins at the **Royal Seal Desk**. Interact with the physical block to open the register; there is no remote-opening key or command.

Craft it with:

| Left | Centre | Right |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Book | Feather | Gold ingot |
| Planks | Green wool | Planks |
| Planks | Planks | Planks |

Place it on a solid base with two blocks of free height.

## Browse the public register

Any player can search the catalogue and see inhabitants' current titles. Titles may be permanent or last **1–365 real days**. A character can hold up to 16 titles at once.

## Create and award a title

With Realm Council installed, management belongs to the **current elected ruler after the oath**. Being an operator does not override that political role. Without Council, an operator designates the responsible person.

The authorised ruler can create a name and description, select a character, and choose an appointment's duration. Existing appointments must be removed before their title definition can be deleted.

## When government changes

Appointments remain when a new ruler takes office. The change affects who may manage future appointments and revocations, rather than removing everyone's honours.

Titles are public roleplay information. They do not grant combat attributes, salary, tax changes, reputation, operator powers, or permissions in unrelated mods.

## Show your honours

[Realm Identity](/wiki/realm-identity/) can display current honours when the document is consulted again. A new physical document is not required for each title change.
